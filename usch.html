USCH · Unified Student Communication Hub

Everything that matters. Less of the noise.

A research-led dashboard concept for messages, deadlines and course resources.

UCC interaction design project · 2025End-to-end UX research & product design · Figma

USCH dashboard prototype. Consolidation represents a proposed experience, not deployed integrations with university systems.

01 / The problem

One degree.
Too many places to check.

Students were navigating messages and deadlines across disconnected services. Eight interviews explored the resulting platform switching, duplicated notifications, and uncertainty about what needed attention first.

The design goal was a consistent home for academic information, with a hierarchy that helps students identify their next action.

02 / My contribution

Listen. Structure. Prototype. Test.

  • Research. Conducted eight interviews and developed empathy maps to understand student communication habits.
  • Information architecture. Organised a unified dashboard around priority actions, messages, calendar events and course resources.
  • Interaction design. Developed wireframes and a high-fidelity Figma experience, including prioritised notifications and Focus Mode.
  • Evaluation. Tested with 45 students across two moderated rounds, using feedback to refine the experience.

03 / Validation

Clarity people could use.

8Research interviews
45Students tested
90%Reported ease of use

Prototype evaluation recorded an 80% task success rate and a 90% ease-of-use score. These describe the study, not measured changes in live student outcomes.

04 / Architecture & next steps

The interface is one part of the system.

Bringing several services into one interface raises questions about identity, permissions, duplicate messages, source ownership and sync behaviour. Those are requirements for a future implementation, not completed backend features of this Figma project.

I would test lower-fidelity navigation earlier, add staff-side research, and use a short diary study to understand actual checking habits. A next technical prototype would validate one authorised integration before expanding to multiple sources.
